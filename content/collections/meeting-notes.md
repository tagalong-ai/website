## Start with your own notes

Write down the points that matter to you while the conversation is happening. A transcript provides the words; your notes capture what you want to remember, question, or follow up on.

Afterward, review the summary and decisions, check speaker names, and choose which action items to export. AI-generated material is useful context to review alongside your own record.

## Choose the right workflow

Use the setup guide for recording and permissions. The product tour shows the meeting workspace, and the privacy guide explains when information stays local and when it is sent for processing or sharing.

Features marked **In QA** on the website are release candidates. They are separate from the capabilities in the current public download.
