## Start with the reason you are switching

A useful Granola alternative should solve a specific gap in your meeting workflow. Do you want ordinary files on your Mac? Local-model transcription? Visual summaries? Tasks in Apple Reminders? Or a free way to record more meetings?

**Publisher disclosure:** This comparison is written by Tagalong, one of the products included. It is a review of current product documentation, not a hands-on performance benchmark or an independent ranking. Sources were checked on September 5, 2026. Tagalong details refer to the public **3.4.1** release; other vendors can change their plans and features.

| Product | Consider it for | Important tradeoff | Starting point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Tagalong** | Local meeting files, personal notes, live AI, visual notes, and selected task export | Mac only; cloud processing powers AI features; advanced workflows require a paid plan | Free: five meetings/month; [plan details](/#pricing) |

Prices and limits are not directly equivalent: five microphone meetings, unlimited recordings, and limited hosted history describe different allowances. Check the workflow you need, not just the price next to the plan name.

## Tagalong: for a meeting library you keep on your Mac

Tagalong connects microphone or conference-audio recording with live transcription, personal notes, AI questions, and a saved meeting workspace. After processing, you can review a summary, decisions, action items, and speaker labels, then edit names. Named speaker identification is part of post-meeting processing; the live transcript should not be confused with final named-speaker attribution.

The useful distinction is the saved library: transcripts, notes, retained audio, and generated visuals are local files. Search reads meeting text on your Mac. AI processing still uses providers, and speaker transcription sends recorded audio for cloud processing. [See the data-flow explanation](/blog/meeting-data-on-your-mac/).

Choose Tagalong if you want your own notes alongside the transcript, [visual meeting summaries](/collections/visual-meeting-notes/), or [action items in Apple Reminders](/blog/meeting-action-items-apple-reminders/). Choose another workflow if you require all processing offline or a browser-based shared meeting workspace.

The Free tier includes five meetings per month, microphone recording, basic summaries, Markdown export, and editable speakers. Pro is **$9.99/month** with managed AI access. The **$199 one-time Private License** unlocks the released Pro features using your own provider keys; provider usage is billed separately. Prices are USD; confirm checkout totals. [Download and pricing](/#pricing).

## MacWhisper: for local-model transcription

MacWhisper documents local models for transcription, offline processing, file transcription, and capture from Mac apps. It is worth evaluating when the transcription engine and handling of audio files are your first priorities. Check which features require Pro and which optional AI choices use an external provider. [MacWhisper’s product documentation](https://www.macwhisper.com/).

## Muesli: for an open-source approach

Muesli describes a Mac-native workflow built around local capture, a reviewable source transcript, exports, and open-source code. It distinguishes that source record from optional AI summarization. Consider it if inspecting the software and controlling the transcript workflow matter more to you than adopting a hosted team workspace. [Muesli’s product explanation](https://muesli.works/granola-alternative).

## Fathom: for a free recording-and-summary workflow

Fathom’s free plan lists unlimited recordings and transcriptions, instant summaries, clips, and search. Its pricing page currently offers both bot capture and bot-free capture, with the latter marked beta for Mac. Do not assume that every advanced AI feature is unlimited on Free; consult the plan matrix for action items and conversational assistance. [Fathom’s pricing and feature matrix](https://www.fathom.ai/pricing).

## When staying with Granola makes sense

Granola’s Basic plan includes AI notes, chat within and across meetings, shared folders, and templates, with limited meeting history. Business adds unlimited history and advanced integrations. Its security documentation describes cloud transcription and hosted notes. If that managed, shared workflow suits your team, changing apps solely for a different feature checklist may add work. [Granola plans](https://www.granola.ai/pricing) and [security details](https://www.granola.ai/security).

## Try one synthetic meeting before moving your workflow

Use the same short, fictional conversation in each app you evaluate. Include a decision, an owner, a date, and an unresolved question. Check whether you can find the source behind the summary, correct a speaker label, recover your original notes, and export the actual fields you need.

For Tagalong, start with the [product tour](/#tour), then [install the free Mac app](/guide). Conference audio, visual notes, enhanced notes, search, and task export are paid features. A subscription trial or purchase should be evaluated against those specific needs; the Free tier does not demonstrate every paid workflow.
